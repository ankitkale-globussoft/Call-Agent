======================================================================================
Doctor's Avliblity API
To set a schedule for a doctor who is only available on specific days, you need to make separate POST requests for each day to /api/v1/schedule/availability.

The day_of_week uses standard Python indexing:

0 = Monday
1 = Tuesday
2 = Wednesday
3 = Thursday
4 = Friday
5 = Saturday
6 = Sunday

Appointment's Booking

Update appointment status :-
            'PENDING', 'CONFIRMED', 'CANCELLED' or 'COMPLETED'

The /appointments/{id}/status endpoint is the control center for the lifecycle of an appointment. It manages the transition from "just booked" to "finished."

Here is who uses it and why:

1. The Patient (To Cancel)
Action: CANCELLED
Why: If a patient realizes they can't make it, they hit this endpoint.
What happens behind the scenes: The system automatically re-opens the slot. It sets is_booked = False for that time block, allowing another patient to book it immediately.
2. The Clinic Staff / Admin (To Confirm or Reschedule)
Action: CONFIRMED or CANCELLED
Why:
Confirmation: Some clinics like to manually "Verify" an appointment before it's official.
Clinic Cancellation: If the doctor suddenly has an emergency, the admin cancels the appointment and notifies the patient.
3. The Doctor (To Complete)
Action: COMPLETED
Why: After the check-up is over, the doctor marks it as COMPLETED.
Value: This is crucial for:
Billing: You only bill for completed appointments.
Medical History: It creates a record that the visit actually happened.
Analytics: The clinic can see how many patients were actually seen vs. how many "No-shows" occurred.
🛡️ Built-in Security Logic:
I have added specific rules to this endpoint so people can't mess with each other's data:

Patients can ONLY change the status to CANCELLED (and only for their own appointments).
Doctors/Admins can change the status to CONFIRMED or COMPLETED.
A Patient cannot mark their own appointment as COMPLETED (they can't "fake" a visit).
This endpoint ensures the "Reality" of the clinic (who showed up, who didn't) matches the "Database."
